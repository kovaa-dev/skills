# On-touch transition from legacy work

This is the ordinary preparation path, not an optional cleanup action. New work uses
[requirements](requirements.md) and [queues](work-queue.md). The PRD/layer-task scheme is
officially legacy and remains operational only for records not yet converted.
For an explicit one-shot transition use [migrate](governance-migration.md).

## Trigger and authority

Before continuing implementation, grooming, closure, or a material edit of a selected
legacy PRD/task, first capture its affected contract and remaining work in the new model.
Apply the same rule when carrying forward a pre-existing contract from an old chat,
checklist, task, or another noncanonical source.

New requirements and clarifications received during current work use
[ongoing capture](requirements.md#questions-during-work). Recording that input alone does
not initiate a legacy conversion or require a separate migration baseline/map/notice.

Reading an unrelated legacy link is not a request to migrate it or the entire backlog.
Convert the selected outcome and the directly necessary shared contracts/prerequisites.
The unit may be a section/range inside a larger source. Reuse existing canonical owners
and conversion mappings; source-wide conversion requires coverage of all its active content.

Authorized project work includes its necessary documentation continuity. Do not require
init, selection of an adoption method, or separate approval just for this bounded
conversion. Respect explicit read-only/no-migration instructions, unavailable write
authority, and binding project rules. In that case provide the proposed capture and exact
limitation; do not claim conversion or continue affected implementation on an uncaptured
or disputed contract. Independent already-authorized work may continue.

## Coverage and reconciliation

Every claimed converted range needs a reviewable source-to-destination accounting.
For a small conversion, keep it inline in the source notice or existing work result;
a full/large migration can use the [coverage form](forms-migration.md). Completeness is
required at either scale, without a mandatory extra file or permanent second registry.

1. **Establish the baseline.** Name the selected sources/ranges and preserve their actual
   version or exact snapshot. Read all content in the claimed ranges, including applicable
   linked constraints/acceptance, not just headings or task summaries. Record unavailable
   sources and out-of-scope remainders explicitly; they are not converted by implication.
2. **Account for meaning.** Identify each independent obligation and its conditions,
   exceptions, negative constraints, applicability, prerequisites, acceptance, open questions,
   decisions, deferred work, and historical results. A source heading/paragraph/item locator
   is sufficient; no new legacy ID or per-line numbering is needed. For each unit, record
   its canonical destination or explicit disposition and reason. Destinations may be R-ID
   and revision, decision/question anchor, description/procedure, or work/result/history.
3. **Preserve distinctions.** Shared destinations are allowed only after checking that all
   contributing conditions and exceptions survive. Similar titles are not proof of a
   duplicate. Replaced/retired meaning needs its accepted reason and successor/boundary;
   a proposal stays proposed. Unconverted residual content retains an exact active source
   route. An unresolved question may be captured at a canonical question anchor without
   inventing an accepted requirement or an answer.
4. **Check both directions.** Compare each baseline unit with its destination to find
   omissions or weakening, then each destination with its sources to detect invented scope,
   acceptance, or verification. Check meaning, not just counts, IDs, links, or a list of
   filenames. Grouped mappings must still account for every contributing unit.
5. **Reconcile changes during work.** Capture newly received/discovered requirements and
   clarifications through [ongoing capture](requirements.md#questions-during-work), with
   their own source/state rather than attributing them to the old baseline. Before closure,
   compare affected source changes since the baseline and account for these new inputs.
   Newly found out-of-scope material keeps a visible source/next question without silently
   expanding the migration or disappearing from the remaining-scope record.

Do not retire/delete a source range or mark it converted until all its meaning has a
verified destination or an accepted historical/inapplicable disposition. Missing/unread
or unmapped content is incomplete coverage. Captured open questions are preserved content:
their unresolved answers affect work readiness, not the truth of that coverage claim.
The final result names the checked baseline/ranges, destinations, new inputs, and remaining
gaps; an independent reader must be able to follow them without reconstructing the chat.
When condensing temporary coverage, keep the checked unit-to-destination mapping,
baseline, and dispositions accessible inline or through a historical result. Condense
working logs, not the ability to establish what went where. This provenance is not a
second live requirement catalog or work queue.

## Convert before continuing

1. **Recover and classify.** Identify the legacy source/ID, actual outcome, accepted
   requirements and decisions, open questions, dependencies, remaining work, and historic
   acceptance/verification. Preserve a recoverable VCS revision, exact copy, or patch
   before replacing source text. Use [coverage and reconciliation](#coverage-and-reconciliation)
   for the baseline and semantic units; do not reconstruct missing facts from memory.
2. **Capture the contract.** Move applicable obligations into the owning specification,
   with stable requirement IDs, applicability/revision, acceptance method, rationale,
   and honest definition/verification state. Reuse matching obligations rather than
   creating one requirement per checkbox. Preserve technical decisions in their owner.
   Record hypotheses as draft/questions and coherent unaccepted alternatives as proposed,
   targeting the current contract. Preserve proven obsolete material as nonbinding history
   with a successor/retirement notice under the [lifecycle](requirements.md#retiring-stale-material);
   conversion must not resurrect it as a current obligation.
3. **Capture actual work.** Add/reuse remaining independently useful outcomes under
   [current queue registration/readiness](work-queue.md). Preserve legacy state as history,
   not proof of current readiness. Research is work with an answer/artifact, not a fake
   product requirement. Map waits to specific supplying work/results; preserve unresolved
   external prerequisites explicitly. Import completed outcomes into queue history with
   the original result/date/limits when this source is being converted.
4. **Switch the selected reading route.** Mark the exact converted sections/ranges and
   link their new owners, applicable queue entries, and results from the old source and
   affected navigation. Only those ranges become historical for current execution.
   For a partial source, add a prominent notice identifying converted ranges and the
   remaining active legacy sections/work. Keep that remainder readable and operational
   under on-touch conversion; do not archive the whole body or retire its remaining work.
   Keep original IDs, closure facts, acceptance, and verification accessible. The converted
   scope must be usable without old task text as required context.
5. **Verify and continue.** Perform the [two-way coverage check](#coverage-and-reconciliation),
   including new inputs received during the work. Verify IDs, redirects, dependencies,
   applicable checks, and discoverability of the unconverted remainder.
   Continue the selected converted scope only in its new canonical records; its old
   formulation/status is historical. Do not duplicate live contracts or execution state.

The coverage mapping is provenance/navigation, not another work state. On repeated entry,
follow it and reuse the existing records; account only for actual new or changed material.
A whole-source converted label requires coverage of every active part, not just the selected
section or its title.

## History and unresolved material

A legacy done checkbox is historical evidence, not fresh proof of an extracted current
requirement. Preserve the source version/date when known and explicitly label missing
version or unverified current behavior. Import cancelled work as cancelled, not delivered.
Do not reopen or invent remaining work for a completed outcome merely to populate a queue.

Preserve each independent obligation, accepted exception, question, decision, meaningful
prerequisite, deferred result, and verification record. A task can map to several
requirements and work entries; several tasks can map to one shared requirement.
Classify current, proposed, and historical applicability before calling two formulations
conflicting. Unresolved current conflicts remain visible; migration does not choose
one by recency, severity, or convenience.

If a necessary prerequisite is still legacy, convert its minimum relevant contract/work
or leave an exact unresolved wait with a source locator. The selected work cannot be
declared ready while that prerequisite's required result is unknown. Do not expand the
conversion into unrelated prerequisites, source corpora, or historical tasks.

## Ending the legacy workflow

Navigation and agent-facing entrypoints must state the new model and this on-touch rule
when they are in the authorized affected scope. Retained old templates/parsers are
compatibility readers, not instructions to generate new task cards. Do not install a
parallel tracker or require a permanent migration-mode flag.

Legacy operational routing ends when no unconverted active work remains in the declared
scope and all relevant contracts have canonical destinations. Preserved historical
tasks may remain archived/redirected without keeping the old workflow alive.
Do not delete them or rewrite their historical meaning without explicit cleanup authority.
